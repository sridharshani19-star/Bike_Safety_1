How the system works

01
Detection. An accelerometer measures impact force and lean angle, and a vibration sensor detects shock.
02
Confirmation. A buzzer sounds for 20 seconds. If the rider is conscious they press the button and the alert is cancelled.
03
Alert. With no response, the location and time go to the family group with ride booking links.
04
Response. Anyone can tap I proceed so the group knows who is going, instead of everyone waiting for someone else.
05
Zone. That spot becomes an accident zone. Riders approaching it are warned by voice at 1 km, 500 m and 200 m.
Sound and voice. Tap Start monitoring on Home once. Phones block audio until you do. Routing uses OSRM and place search uses OpenStreetMap. No paid service is used anywhere in this app.
